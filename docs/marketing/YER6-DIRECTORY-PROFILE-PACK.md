## Short English description

YER6 provides geotechnical engineering and field execution services across
Türkiye, specializing in ground improvement, jet grouting, deep soil mixing,

## Long English description

YER6 Ground Improvement & Geotechnical Engineering supports contractors,
design offices, industrial investors and property owners with project-specific
geotechnical solutions across Türkiye. Our services include jet grouting, deep
soil mixing (DSM), bored piles, micropiles, ground anchors, excavation support,
site investigation, soil drilling and foundation underpinning. We review ground
conditions and structural loads together, then develop the appropriate method,
equipment plan and execution sequence. YER6 manages site assessment,
quantities, field execution, quality control and technical reporting for
residential, industrial, warehouse, infrastructure and deep-excavation projects.
